FrameSheet 3.0.0 is a rebuild. It reproduces [MoviePrint](https://github.com/fakob/MoviePrint_v004) 0.2.23 as a native app for Apple Silicon on macOS 27: many movies at once, interval, shot-detection and face sheets, editable thumbs and scenes, a player view, and export as PNG/JPG, JSON and EDL. It replaces v2, a settings-to-one-image contact sheet generator.

## Download

`FrameSheet-3.0.0.zip` below holds the app, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Unzip it, move FrameSheet.app to Applications and open it.

## Features

**Movies and sheets**
- Add movies with the + button, the A key, a drop on the window, or Open With. Each gets a default interval sheet of 16 thumbs in 4 columns.
- Many sheets per movie: interval, shot detection and faces, in grid or timeline view. Rename, duplicate, convert to interval, delete, and undo up to 50 edits.
- Per-movie rotation, cropping and aspect ratio. They apply to the grid, scrub, player, scans and saved images alike.
- A moved movie shows "Movie is missing" with Locate…; Replace movie… relinks a movie and keeps its sheets.

**Editing thumbs and scenes**
- Hover a thumb for IN, OUT, insert before or after, EXPAND, HIDE and SAVE.
- Drag a thumb's handle to scrub to another frame, or to add one before or after; reorder thumbs by dragging.
- Shot detection scans a movie once (mean or histogram) and streams the scenes into a timeline. Cut and merge scenes, expand a scene into its own sheet, and see the detection chart (F).

**Faces**
- Add faces sheet finds faces with Apple Vision (every 10th or 100th frame, or the thumbs shown) and makes a sheet of the frames with faces.
- Sort by face count, size or confidence, filter by count and size, and rescan with new thresholds (Apply).

**Player view**
- Plays the movie with its sheet below: step by frame, thumb or cut, and cut, merge or change thumbs at the current frame.

**Saving and exchange**
- Save sheet as PNG or JPG (up to 16384 px), with an optional header, file-name templates and each thumb as its own image.
- Save All Sheets saves every sheet of every movie in turn.
- A saved PNG carries the sheet in MoviePrint-compatible text chunks.
- Export JSON, and EDL (CMX3600) for shot sheets. Import a PNG or JSON saved by FrameSheet or MoviePrint 0.2.x.

**Decoding**
- AVFoundation with hardware decoding first, ffmpeg for what AVFoundation cannot read.
- Frames AVFoundation cannot decode exactly (open-GOP H.264) are read from the previous GOP, or fetched with ffmpeg when "Fetch exact frames with ffmpeg" is on (default).

**Menus and toolbar**
- Every toolbar command is also in the File, Edit, View or Sheet menu, most with a ⌘ shortcut; the single-letter keys stay. The toolbar is customizable, and face detection settings are in Sheet → Face Detection….

## Fixed before release

Three pre-release audits found problems that are fixed in this release. Among them:
- A movie list that cannot be read at launch is left untouched instead of being saved over.
- A cancelled or timed-out ffmpeg run is stopped even if it ignores the request.
- Replace movie… and Locate… are refused while a scan runs, so its results cannot land on the new file.
- Thumbs, prints, the scrub and the player show exactly the frame they name; a frame that cannot be decoded is refused instead of being stored.
- Importing JSON or PNG data with out-of-range numbers no longer crashes the app.
- Saving with Overwrite off never overwrites, and Save All reports sheets saved with frames missing.
- Duplicating a shot sheet keeps its scenes.
- Later rounds: ffmpeg stream and timing handling, the pixels of saved prints, playback after a source change, and a release built from a clean snapshot that is checked for private paths before it is published.

## Requirements

- macOS 27 or later on Apple Silicon.
- Optional: FFmpeg 5.1 or later, for WebM, MKV, WMV and HEVC tagged `hev1`, and for fetching exact frames from open-GOP files (for example `brew install ffmpeg`). FrameSheet finds it in the usual install locations, or choose it in Settings (⌘,).

## Not included

- MoviePrint's guide layout (paper aspect ratios), face grouping (unique faces, occurrence, same-face filter) and age and gender estimation.
- Audio when a movie plays through ffmpeg (files AVFoundation cannot read). Movies AVFoundation plays have audio.

## Known issues

The full list, with workarounds, is in [docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md](https://github.com/kni927/FrameSheet/blob/v3.0.0/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md). The main ones:

- Adding a large un-indexed file that only ffmpeg reads (for example WebM without cues) can take a while, without progress, while its duration is found.
- On the ffmpeg path the frame count is estimated from the duration, so the last interval thumb may land about 0.5 s early.
- All AVFoundation decodes run one at a time. Many parallel decodes once triggered a kernel panic in the macOS hardware decoder driver.
- Open-GOP H.264 files decode only partly with AVFoundation. Without ffmpeg, such thumbs show the nearest decodable frame with a marker.
- Scans run slower while other apps load the machine heavily (a remote-screen app, a browser using much memory).
- ffmpeg 5.1 or later is needed for the files ffmpeg reads.
