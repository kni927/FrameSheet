## Background

MoviePrint's player view (`docs/V3_SPEC.md` §9, keys in §8) is where frames are chosen precisely and where shot sheets are cut and merged frame by frame. T05 left two items for it: Cut and Jump to cut should use the player's frame, and Return in the timeline is a player key. ## Goal

The owner can open the player view for the selected thumb or scene, step frame by frame, play with audio, cut and merge scenes or change and add thumbs at the current frame, and save the current frame, as §8 and §9 describe.

## Scope

### In scope

1. **Layout (§9):** window view switch standard ↔ player (key 2, toolbar button "Show player view", double-click on a thumb or scene); video on top, the sheet as one horizontal strip below (thumbs, or scene bars at 0.3 px per frame); timeline bar with playhead and IN/OUT; current frame number; Space toggles the player view, and on shot sheets toggles cut/merge vs change-thumb mode.
2. **Frame strip (§9):** 20 vertical slices of consecutive frames around the current frame (19 in grid mode); middle slice about 1/4 width (1/3 for an odd count), halving outward; 1 px gaps, 8 px at a cut. Decode the run of consecutive frames with one sequential read through `DecodeGate` rather than one request per slice; cache it while stepping.
3. **Stepping and playback:** ←/→ ±10, ⇧ ±1, ⌥ ±100, ⇧⌥ previous/next cut (timeline) or thumb (grid); the matching buttons (previous, −100, −10, −1, +1, +10, +100, next); playback with audio (AVPlayer) that stops on the exact frame. Every shown frame is exact: use decision 25's steps for paused frames; open-GOP files must not show a wrong frame (say what the player shows while playing them).
4. **Editing at the current frame:** shot sheets: CUT here / MERGE at a cut (Return, and the button); Jump to cut start/end and the timeline's Cut use the player's frame (T05 follow-ups). Interval sheets: CHANGE TO the current frame / ADD BEFORE / ADD AFTER (Return, ⇧/⌥ + Return, and the buttons). All through the undoable path.
5. **Save frame (S):** saves the current frame at original resolution with the single-thumb naming scheme (`[MN].[ME]-frame[FN]`), transform applied.
6. **ffmpeg-routed files:** the player works for them too (frame strip and stepping through the ffmpeg path; playback may be unavailable: then say so in the UI).

- Editing at the frame (one undo step each): CUT / MERGE on shot sheets, CHANGE / ADD BEFORE / ADD AFTER on interval sheets, change-only in thumb mode on shot sheets; inexact frames refused; toolbar Cut and Jump to cut use the player's frame while it is open.
- Save frame at original resolution with the single-thumb naming, named by the decoded frame.
- Playback holds `DecodeGate` (AVPlayer and the ffmpeg pipe), so no strip, still, thumb or scan decode runs beside it; a scan pauses playback; open-GOP playback starting on a leading frame keeps the exact still until the GOP's keyframe.
- ffmpeg-routed files: video-only playback through one rawvideo pipe paced by pts (frames dropped when late, never ahead), labeled "Playback using ffmpeg · no audio".
